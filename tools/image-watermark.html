How to add watermarks

  1. Drop in the images you want to protect.
  2. Type the watermark text, pick a position or tiling, adjust size and opacity.
  3. Press Add watermark and download the results.

Tips

  • Ownership proof: use the tile mode with 20–30% opacity — it survives cropping and is hard to remove.
  • Shared drafts: a single corner mark with the word DRAFT keeps documents readable.
  • Light photos: white text with a soft black shadow stays visible on any background.

Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Watermarks are drawn with the Canvas API inside your browser. Images never leave your device.

Can someone remove my watermark?

Any visible watermark can technically be edited out. Dense low-opacity tiling is by far the hardest to remove cleanly and is what we recommend for proof of ownership.

Can I use a logo image instead of text?

Not yet — the current tool stamps text. Image watermarks are on the roadmap.
