How it works

  1. Drop in any images you want to convert.
  2. Pick the target format, quality and optional max size.
  3. Hit Convert and download the results — individually or as a ZIP.

Which format should I choose?

  • WebP is the best default for the web: sharp graphics and photos at a fraction of the size.
  • JPEG is ideal for photos where maximum compatibility matters (email, older apps).
  • PNG is lossless and supports transparency — right for logos, screenshots and anything that must stay pixel-perfect.

Frequently asked questions

Does converting lose quality?

Converting to JPEG or WebP uses lossy compression — you control it with the quality slider (default 90%). Converting to PNG is lossless. Your original files are never modified or uploaded.

Why convert PNG to WebP?

WebP typically makes screenshots and graphics 60–90% smaller than PNG at the same visual quality — faster websites and more free disk space.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. The whole conversion runs locally in your browser tab. Nothing is sent to any server, and most tools keep working even if you go offline after loading the page.
