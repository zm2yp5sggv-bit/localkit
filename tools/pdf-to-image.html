How to convert a PDF to images

  1. Drop in your PDF.
  2. Choose PNG or JPG and a resolution (2× is a good default).
  3. Press Convert to images — download individually or as a ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

Will the exported images be sharp?

Yes — pick 2× for crisp on-screen use or 3× for print. PNG keeps text razor sharp; JPG suits photo-heavy pages.

Why convert PDF pages to images?

Common uses: previews and thumbnails, chat apps that mangle PDFs, slides you want as pictures, and pages that must never be edited again.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Rendering happens entirely in your browser with PDF.js — the document never leaves your device.
