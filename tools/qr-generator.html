Wi-Fi QR codes, explained

Choose the Wi-Fi login type and enter your network details — phones that scan the code connect automatically, no password dictation needed. Handy for guests, cafés, offices and rental properties.

Frequently asked questions

Do the QR codes expire or stop working?

Never. These are static QR codes — the content is encoded directly in the image, not behind a redirect service that could shut down. They work forever and are fine for print.

PNG or SVG download?

PNG is best for screens, chat apps and documents. SVG is vector-based — it scales to any size without blurring, ideal for print, signage and engraving.

Is any data sent to a server?

No. The QR code is built in your browser, so whatever you encode — Wi-Fi passwords included — stays on your device.
