How to compress images

  1. Drop your images onto the box above (or click it to pick files).
  2. Choose a format, quality level and optional maximum size in pixels.
  3. Press Compress — each result shows the original vs. new size.
  4. Download files one by one, or grab everything as a ZIP.

Tips for smaller files

  • Photos: JPEG at 70–85% quality is usually indistinguishable from the original and 3–10× smaller.
  • Screenshots and graphics: choose WebP — it stays sharp while cutting size dramatically.
  • PNG files: PNG is lossless, so the quality slider won't change it. Convert to WebP for real savings, or reduce the max dimension.
  • For the web: resizing to the actual display width (e.g. 1600 px) often saves more than quality tweaks alone.

Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Compression happens entirely inside your browser using the Canvas API. Your images never leave your device — you can even disconnect from the internet after loading the page.

Why is my PNG barely smaller after compressing?

PNG is a lossless format, so the quality setting doesn't affect it. To shrink PNG files dramatically, pick WebP or JPEG as the output format, or lower the max dimension.

Is there a file count or size limit?

No artificial limits. Because everything runs on your own hardware, the practical limit is your device's memory. On phones, batches of a few hundred photos work fine.
