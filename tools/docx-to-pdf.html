How to convert DOCX to PDF

  1. Drop in a Word .docx file.
  2. Press Convert to PDF — each page of the document becomes a PDF page.
  3. Download the finished PDF.

What to expect

The document is rendered in your browser and each page is placed into the PDF as a high-resolution image, so text is not selectable in the output. Simple documents (letters, reports, resumes) convert very reliably; exotic fonts and complex layouts may shift slightly. For pixel-perfect legal output, export from Word itself.

Frequently asked questions

Is my document uploaded to a server?

No. The .docx is parsed in your browser and the PDF is built on your device — confidential documents stay with you.

Will the PDF look exactly like in Word?

Layout, fonts and images are reproduced faithfully for typical documents. Very complex formatting (exact print typography, some table features) may differ slightly — check the result before sending.

Which files are supported?

Modern .docx files (Word 2007+). The legacy binary .doc format is not supported — re-save the file as .docx in Word first.
