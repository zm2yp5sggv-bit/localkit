How to split a PDF

  1. Drop in your PDF.
  2. To pull out specific pages, keep Extract page range and type ranges like 1-3,7,12-15.
  3. To explode the document, choose Every page — you'll get one PDF per page in a ZIP.
  4. Press Split and download.

Common uses

  • Send only the relevant pages of a long report to a client.
  • Remove sensitive pages before sharing — since splitting is local, the original never leaves your machine.
  • Chapter-per-file workflows for scanned books and lecture notes.

Frequently asked questions

How do I specify which pages to extract?

Comma-separated ranges, e.g. 1-3,7,12-15. Numbers refer to physical pages of the PDF starting at 1 — the same numbers your PDF reader shows.

Can I split every page into a separate PDF?

Yes — pick the Every page mode. All pages are exported as individual PDFs packed into one ZIP archive.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens in your browser tab. For confidential documents that's exactly what you want — nothing is ever transmitted.
